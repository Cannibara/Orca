1. The map, at the top

Changed

Peers come from the famous-funds list the fund is in (Tiger cubs, Tech & growth…). A fund in two lists gets a switch. A fund in no list would use funds of a similar size. New: "Show its path" draws where the fund sat on each rolling 3-year window, so readers see if its style changed.

What kind of investor?
Yearly points gained vs the market, split in two · since 2014 · estimate

Units. "pts a year" means percentage points of yearly return: +2 pts = 2% a year more, e.g. 12% a year instead of 10%. Measured on every dollar they put in since 2014. Across: how much its stocks beat their own sectors. Up: how much its sectors beat the whole market. The grey cross near zero is "too close to call": a gap that small is often chance. Dot size = number of picks.

    4. Which decisions made the gap

    Changed

    Sectors vs market leaves stock picks out on purpose: each sector's dot is that sector's whole index, bought and sold on the fund's dates, so only the choice of sector counts. Stock picks are the other tab. New in both tabs: the money they put in is a bar instead of words, and the last column multiplies the two (share of money × how much it beat by). So a big bet with a small edge can outrank a small bet with a big one.
